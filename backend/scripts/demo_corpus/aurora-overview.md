# Project Aurora — Community Microgrid Overview

Project Aurora is a community-scale renewable microgrid operated by the Rivergate
Energy Cooperative in the town of Millbrook. It was commissioned in the third
quarter of 2025 and supplies clean electricity to approximately 1,800 households.

## Key facts

- Total generation capacity: 4.2 MW.
- Households served: 1,800.
- Commissioned: Q3 2025.
- Total project budget: 12.4 million USD.
- Operator: Rivergate Energy Cooperative.
- Location: Millbrook.

## Purpose

Aurora was built to reduce Millbrook's reliance on the regional grid during peak
demand and to keep power flowing to critical facilities — the medical clinic, the
water-treatment plant, and two emergency shelters — during outages. When the
regional grid is available, Aurora runs grid-tied and exports surplus energy;
during an outage it islands and powers the critical-facilities circuit on its own.

## Status

As of the end of 2025, Aurora had operated for one full quarter and met its
first-year availability target of 99.2 percent. The cooperative has approved a
Phase 2 study to evaluate doubling battery storage.
